About KTC Tea

What started as a small family packing unit in Veraval has grown into a trusted name across Gujarat and beyond. We source only the finest Crush-Tear-Curl leaves, blend them to our own recipe, and pack every batch ourselves — the same way since 1986.

🌿
Sourced from Premier Tea Gardens
We work directly with Assam and Darjeeling CTC estates. No middlemen, no compromises — only leaves that pass our four-decade-old quality check.
☕
Blended to a Family Recipe
Our two blends — KTC Gold and KTC Tea — have remained unchanged since 1986. The same colour, the same strength, the same morning ritual for thousands of families.
📦
Packed Fresh, Delivered Fast
Every pack is sealed airtight at our Veraval unit and dispatched within 24 hours. Free delivery across India on orders above ₹1000.
